# Teeter

**An AI bot trading a real $100 on the Nasdaq. Every trade, explained.**

Live: https://trader-tracker-murex.vercel.app

Teeter is a public log of a small, real-money experiment. An automated AI agent trades a
~$100 brokerage account using only two funds: TQQQ (moves about 3x the Nasdaq-100's daily
move) and SQQQ (about 3x the opposite). The name comes from the seesaw: the bot tips one way
or the other, and you can watch it happen.

What the site shows:
- the account's current value and how today went
- an animated replay of the account value, with every buy and sell marked
- each trade's reason in plain English, with the bot's original note under "Details"
- best and worst trades: sells matched to their buys (FIFO per fund), sortable by profit,
  %, date, time held or fund
- news the bot itself wrote down in its research notes (nothing is added from outside)

## Data
`data/trades.json` and `data/equity.json` are generated by `scripts/export.py` from the bot's
private records (`TRADER_DIR=... python3 scripts/export.py`). They never contain account numbers, order or
instrument ids, cash or buying power, deposits, or raw broker data.

Per-trade fields: `time, date, symbol, side, shares, price, notional, order_type, session,
sleeve, why` (plain English), `details` (bot's note), `result` (on sells), `news` (bot research
notes seen before the trade) and, when the bot logs it, `logged` (`reason, plain, score,
signals, news[{headline, source, url, time}]`).

## Brand
Seesaw mark + Newsreader wordmark (`logo.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `brand/`). The social image is
stored as base64 in `brand/og.png.b64` and decoded at build time (see `vercel.json`).

can lose value quickly. Past results say nothing about the future.
